`asActor()` takes a verified signed delegation (`{ delegation }`), which counts on the principal's side of the permission check beside grants naming the principal, evaluated as a grant would be (write implies read) and never reaching the families that govern authority. It refuses a session carrying a `delegationChain` without a delegation, or with one verified from a different chain. `verifyAuthChallenge()` takes a `verifyDid` callback that, when given, decides every DID. A `DidCredential` may carry a `delegationChain` for the handshake to present, `parseAuthTokenBody(body, { delegations: true })` accepts one (and refuses it otherwise), and a `TokenSession` carries the `delegationChain` a delegated token was issued on. New `_revocation@1` system type: a Record naming a delegation's CID revokes it when written by the owner or by an issuer upstream in its chain, recoverable like a grant; `Stack.isDelegationRevoked(cid, issuers)` answers whether one does. New exported types: `SignedDelegation`, `RecordVerb`, `DidSignatureVerifier` and `RevocationContent`.
