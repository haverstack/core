`asActor()` takes a verified signed delegation (`{ delegation }`), which counts on the principal's side of the permission check beside grants naming the principal, for the record verbs and families it allows; it refuses a session issued on a delegation chain when no delegation is passed. `verifyAuthChallenge()` takes a `verifyDid` callback that, when given, decides every DID. A `DidCredential` may carry a `delegation` chain for the handshake to present, `parseAuthTokenBody(body, { delegation: true })` accepts `delegation` (and refuses it otherwise), and a `TokenSession` carries the `delegationChain` a delegated token was issued on. New `_revocation@1` system type: a Record naming a delegation's CID revokes it when written by the owner or by an issuer upstream in its chain, recoverable like a grant; `Stack.isDelegationRevoked(cid, issuers)` answers whether one does. New exported types: `SignedDelegation`, `DelegatedVerb`, `DidSignatureVerifier` and `RevocationContent`.
